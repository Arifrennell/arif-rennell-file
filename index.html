Responsible Repair & Safety

Use these resources lawfully. Work only on devices you own or are explicitly authorized to service. Do not use tools or instructions to access another person's data, bypass security without authorization, or perform unlawful activity.

  • Back up important data before firmware or storage operations whenever possible.
  • Verify the exact model and firmware build; incorrect files can damage a device or erase data.
  • Use official documentation and trusted, security-scanned downloads.
  • Educational materials are general information; results depend on the device, software version and user actions.
